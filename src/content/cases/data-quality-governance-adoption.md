summary: Led key parts of a regulatory-driven Data Quality and Data Governance transformation, connecting governance, issue management, business requirements, management reporting and adoption across business and technical teams.
context: The wider data programme was driven by regulatory expectations around data governance, quality and management information. Existing Data Governance and Data Quality teams therefore needed their scope, processes and reporting tailored to support those regulatory requirements.
challenge: 'The organisation needed to translate regulatory expectations into practical governance: clear roles and councils, structured Data Quality issue management, prioritised remediation, reliable management information and consistent adoption across business and technical teams.'
contribution:
- Led key parts of the Data Quality and Data Governance transformation, particularly change, adoption, business analysis, stakeholder alignment and management reporting.
- Helped set up and refine governance councils.
- Helped define roles and responsibilities across Data Owners, Data Stewards, business stakeholders and technical teams.
- Defined how Data Quality issues should be logged in Jira, including the business fields required for effective assessment and remediation.
- Established escalation logic and biweekly follow-up for prioritised backlog.
- Supported budgeting and resource considerations around remediation and maintained management visibility on progress and budget.
- Trained users on the issue-management approach.
- Supported Collibra governance workflows, including how users could claim and maintain business terms and how ownership processes should operate.
- Defined and helped shape Data Quality KPIs for senior management.
- Presented progress to management and C-level stakeholders.
- Helped translate technical team needs to senior management and management expectations back into technical requirements.
- Supported requirements gathering for the data platform and reporting layer and identified the data needed to start.
- Acted as the bridge between technical delivery and upper management.
