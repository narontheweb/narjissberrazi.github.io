## Context
A Belgian financial institution had a large portfolio of Data Quality issues but lacked a consistent business approach to prioritisation, accountability, impact and follow-up.

## My contribution
- Led key parts of the Data Quality and Data Governance transformation.
- Focused particularly on change and adoption, business analysis, stakeholder alignment and management reporting.
- Defined requirements and KPIs.
- Translated technical Data Quality signals into business-relevant management information.
- Supported prioritisation, training, communication and governance routines.
- Connected technical teams, business stakeholders, data owners and stewards, and management.

## Evidence
- ~300 issues in the portfolio
- 40 prioritised
- ~€11M estimated combined impact across the 40 prioritised issues only
- Improved executive visibility and accountability

**Estimated impact relates to the 40 prioritised issues only.**

## From technical signal to management action
1. Technical DQ signals
2. Business interpretation
3. Impact
4. Prioritisation
5. Ownership
6. Adoption
7. Remediation

## Takeaway
Turning technical Data Quality information into business ownership and management action.
