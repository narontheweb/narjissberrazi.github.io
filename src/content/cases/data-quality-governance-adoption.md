summary: Helped operationalise regulatory Data Governance and Data Quality requirements across the bank — redesigning governance, issue-management and reporting practices while driving change and adoption across business and technical teams.
context: A bank-wide data transformation programme was accelerated by European supervisory expectations around risk data aggregation and reporting, with BCBS 239 / RDARR as core reference points. Existing Data Governance and Data Quality capabilities needed to evolve to provide stronger ownership, traceability, issue management and management oversight across multiple domains in the bank.
challenge: 'Regulatory expectations had to be converted into practical ways of working across multiple domains in the bank: clearer Data Owner and Data Steward responsibilities, operational governance councils, consistent Data Quality issue management, prioritised remediation and reliable C-level reporting.'
contribution:
- Led key parts of the Data Quality and Data Governance transformation, with a particular focus on change and adoption, stakeholder alignment, business analysis and management reporting.
- Helped set up and refine operational governance councils.
- Defined and clarified roles and responsibilities across Data Owners, Data Stewards, business stakeholders and technical teams.
- Defined how Data Quality issues should be logged in Jira, including the business information required to assess, prioritise and remediate issues effectively.
- Established escalation logic and biweekly follow-up for the prioritised backlog.
- Supported remediation budgeting / resource considerations and maintained management visibility on progress and budget.
- Trained users on the issue-management approach.
- Supported Collibra governance workflows, including how users could claim and maintain business terms and how ownership processes should operate.
- Helped define Data Quality KPIs for senior management.
- Presented progress to management and C-level stakeholders.
- Translated technical-team needs into management decisions and management expectations back into practical requirements.
- Supported requirements gathering for the data platform / reporting layer and identified the data required to get started.
- Acted as a bridge between technical delivery teams and upper management.
