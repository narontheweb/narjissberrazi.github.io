summary: Led the sustainability-reporting workstream across 31 reporting entities in multiple countries, coordinating Environmental, Social and Governance data, regulatory interpretation, reporting governance, audit evidence and delivery of a ~200-page sustainability report.
context: A global postal and logistics group needed to operationalise first-wave CSRD reporting across 31 reporting entities in multiple countries. The programme required fragmented Environmental, Social and Governance data to be translated into a consistent, auditable reporting process aligned with evolving ESRS requirements.
challenge: 'The challenge combined regulation, data and organisational complexity: hundreds of Environmental, Social and Governance datapoints had to be interpreted consistently, ownership coordinated across countries and functions, assumptions and evidence documented, data quality challenged and the final disclosures made traceable enough to withstand external audit.'
contribution:
- Led the sustainability-reporting workstream.
- Established governance, reporting routines and ways of working across the sustainability team and participating entities.
- Coordinated Environmental, Social and Governance data collection and reporting across 31 reporting entities in multiple countries.
- Worked across 100+ stakeholders.
- Developed reporting templates and entity guidance.
- Used a Kanban-based delivery approach to organise reporting activities, manage dependencies and maintain transparency on progress across the workstream.
- Coordinated evidence collection for external audit.
- Answered auditor questions from EY.
- Followed up auditor findings and coordinated actions with the team.
- Challenged assumptions, supporting documentation and reporting evidence.
- Supported Data Quality throughout the reporting process.
- Led delivery of the main sustainability report.
- Designed and built an automated CSRD readiness application that linked ESRS regulatory requirements to reporting datapoints, tracked coverage and gaps, and gave the team a structured view of readiness throughout the reporting cycle.
- Leveraged the application during the engagement to monitor reporting preparedness and identify gaps.
