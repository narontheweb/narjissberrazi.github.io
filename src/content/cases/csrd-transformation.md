summary: Led the sustainability-reporting workstream across 31 reporting entities in multiple countries, coordinating governance, data collection, regulatory compliance, audit evidence and delivery of a ~200-page sustainability report.
context: A large European postal and logistics group needed to operationalise CSRD reporting across 31 reporting entities in multiple countries while requirements, interpretations, responsibilities and data availability were still evolving.
challenge: The programme required a consistent reporting approach across countries and entities, traceable evidence for ESRS datapoints, coordinated assumptions and documentation, high-quality data collection and successful navigation of an external audit.
contribution:
- Led the sustainability-reporting workstream.
- Established governance, reporting routines and ways of working across the sustainability team and participating entities.
- Coordinated data collection and reporting across 31 reporting entities in multiple countries.
- Worked across 100+ stakeholders.
- Developed reporting templates and entity guidance.
- Coordinated evidence collection for external audit.
- Answered auditor questions from EY.
- Followed up auditor findings and coordinated actions with the team.
- Challenged assumptions, supporting documentation and reporting evidence.
- Supported Data Quality throughout the reporting process.
- Led delivery of the main sustainability report.
- Built an automated CSRD readiness application that mapped regulatory requirements to ESRS datapoints and helped assess whether reporting requirements were covered.
- Leveraged the automated application within the engagement to monitor reporting preparedness and identify gaps.
