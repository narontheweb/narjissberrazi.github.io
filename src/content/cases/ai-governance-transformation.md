## Context
A Flemish public-sector infrastructure organisation was progressing its broader digital and data transformation while AI opportunities were emerging without a shared governance model, clear ownership or a consistent prioritisation process.

## Challenge
AI ambition existed, but governance, decision rights, oversight, working structures and implementation priorities still needed to be translated into a practical operating model.

## My contribution
- Played a leading role in AI strategy and governance.
- Helped establish the AI working group.
- Shaped roles, responsibilities and decision rights.
- Assessed 10 AI use cases and supported prioritisation across value, feasibility, risk and readiness.
- Developed the roadmap and management decision materials.
- Connected business and IT stakeholders.
- Supported change-management and resource-planning discussions.
- Helped translate responsible-AI principles into practical governance.

## Visible evidence
- 10 AI use cases assessed
- AI working structure established
- Governance and decision rights shaped
- Prioritised roadmap created
